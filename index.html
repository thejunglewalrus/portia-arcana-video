Why this build is hot right now

About 4 months ago, a patch dramatically buffed the Stack Hold core. To understand why it matters, you have to understand how Norma Lineance (the noble-lineage class identity used by Emperor) works: triggering its Fate reduces the current cooldown of skills like Riku Jansae Blade by 10% — a current-CD reduction, not a flat one.

The old core, Emperor's Heart, triggered Fate when you used a skill. Stack Hold instead triggers Fate when you play a card.

The catch: if you hold cards back to time them with skills, you inevitably overflow Aden — you can only hold 2 cards. Every card you can't hold = a Fate trigger you didn't get = cooldown reduction lost.

The NA meta solution: extreme card draw

NA top players are now running max-card-draw setups:

  • Dangri: drop Enhanced Strike, run Quick Draw 60%
  • Evoke: drop Quick Strike, run Quick Draw + Comic Addiction tripod combo

The goal is to pump card supply so high that you never overflow. Quick-draw tripoded skills generate a card and Aden simultaneously, so casting Evoke or Dethrone refills Aden with a tiny delay you can't track by eye. And because Norma Lineance reduces current cooldown, every 0.1s you wait to play a card is benefit lost — so you play them immediately.

The technique: mouse-button spam

You can't hit Z/X with your left hand while it's on QWER/ASDF — physically impossible, even pros can't do it. Macros would solve it but…

Macros are cheating. We're good users, we play by hand.

The trick:

  • In keybinds, set Z's alt key to a mouse extra-button.
  • Mash that button constantly with your right hand while skilling with the left.

On a target dummy you can see it work — the first card slot is never visible because cards are burned the instant they're drawn. Just mash mouse-button → X → mouse-button → X. It's mystery cards: you don't see them, your viewers don't see them, nobody knows what's getting played.
